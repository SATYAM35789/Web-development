Authentication and Authorization
Authentication : 
 - kisi bhi company/ bank ke ander jana hai toh phale check hota hai ki aap kon ho? employ ho ki nahi,
   aapka bankaccount exist or not.
 - aapka userid and password aapka Authorization hai.
 -login karne ka access hai ki nahi. 
 ex authcontroller.js mai sign() method.
 - ye ek process hai jiske through hum pata karte hai, ki aap kon ho, aap exist karte ho ki nahi or
   aapka ke pass data/application ka access hai ki nahi. 

   Common Types of Authentication:- 
   - something you know :-  username and password pata hai or able to answer the security based question.
   -something you have: - Physical devices jaise ki smart card, USB security tokens explict
   - something you are:- Biometric Authentication or aise Physical characterstices jo permit karne ke liye allow 
     karti hai. 

 Authorization:
Aane de raha toh aapka kya kya karne ki access hai, wo aata hai Authorizationmai. 
  - login karne ke baad aap kya kya kar sakte ho. There are different roles defined in Authorization.
  - not do this in this video but will understand the meaning. 
  - process of determining kya aapke pass revelent access(permission/privilages) hai protected resources ko use karne ke liye. 

 So pahle kya karna chahiye Authentication or Authorization :- 
   - You cannot authorized the user or services before identifying them, Authentication always comes before the 
     Authorization.
   - ex email account pahle username aur password ke basis par login karte means authenticate hote hai , phir
     login hone ke baad send or recives message ko access karte hai.
